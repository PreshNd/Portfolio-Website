FAQs

Common questions

Everything you need to know before you book.

What does each package include?
Every package includes return flights, 3-star or higher hotel, airport transfers and listed activities. Breakfast is included where specified.
Are flights always included?
Yes. All packages include return flights, booked and ticketed for you. Flight pricing is confirmed at the time of your quote.
Can I customise my package?
Absolutely. Our travel team will tailor the itinerary to your preferences — just mention it in your special requests when submitting.
How long does it take to get a quote?
We aim to send your personalised quote within 24–48 hours of receiving your request.
What hotel will I get?
We allocate 3-star or similar properties based on availability at your travel dates. Final hotel is confirmed with your quote.
What's the cancellation policy?
Cancellation terms vary by booking window. Full details are included in your quote. Generally, cancellations 30+ days before travel attract no penalty beyond admin fees.
Do you assist with visa applications?
Yes! We offer visa assistance for popular destinations including Dubai, Schengen, UK and more. Our team guides you through the required documents and process.
How much deposit do I need to pay?
A deposit of 30–50% of the total package price is typically required to confirm your booking. Full payment is due at least 14 days before departure.
Can I travel as a solo traveller?
Absolutely. All packages are available for solo travellers. Pricing shown is per person and our team can recommend the best options for solo travel.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers, debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard) and USSD payments. All transactions are processed securely in Naira.
Are travel insurance options available?
Yes. We strongly recommend travel insurance and can arrange comprehensive cover for your trip, including medical, trip cancellation and baggage protection.
What if flight prices change before I confirm?
Quoted prices are held for 24–48 hours. If you confirm and pay your deposit within this window, your price is locked in. After that, availability and pricing may change.
